RAW MATERIAL COSTING – PER KG ONLY The Raw Material Costing master section must calculate raw material costs per KG only. Wastage = 5%. Usable quantity = 95 KG. Total purchase cost = ₹10,000. Effective raw material cost = ₹10,000 ÷ 95 = ₹105.26 per usable KG. Do not simply add 5% to the raw material cost. Handle input GST correctly: - If ITC is eligible, exclude recoverable input GST from product cost. - If ITC is not eligible, include non-creditable GST in product cost. ### Separate Pack-Wise Raw Material Costing Create a separate Pack-Wise Raw Material Costing section. COSTING AND PRICING CALCULATION LOGIC The application must use one consistent calculation engine for all products. All percentage-based costs must have clearly defined calculation bases. Do not apply percentages to arbitrary values. Distinguish between margin and markup. Example: Cost = ₹100. Selling Price = ₹125. Markup = 25%. Margin = 20%. Margins should default to selling-price-based calculations, with optional markup-on-cost settings where required. GST must be calculated on the relevant taxable selling value. GST is not profit. Example: Taxable selling value = ₹120. GST at 5% = ₹6. Final price including GST = ₹126. All calculations must be consistent across: - Product costing - Cost-to-MRP - Reverse MRP-to-Grams - Individual pouch pricing - Ladi pricing - Distributor price lists - Google Sheets --- ## 11. COST-TO-MRP MODE – REGULAR PRODUCTS For regular products: The user enters: - Product - Variety - Raw Material - Pack Type - Pack Weight in grams - Applicable product-specific costs and overrides The application fetches all default settings from Google Sheets. Automatically calculate: - Raw Material Cost - Wastage - Manufacturing Cost - Processing Cost - Grinding Cost, if applicable - Packaging Cost - Label and Printing Cost - Freight - Marketing - Marketing Fund - Scheme Recovery - Manufacturer Margin - Distributor Margin - Retailer Margin - GST - Final MRP The user must NOT manually enter MRP in this mode. MRP must be calculated automatically. Allow multiple pack sizes and packaging types in one product entry. --- ## 12. REVERSE MRP-TO-GRAMS MODE – REGULAR PRODUCTS ONLY For regular products, provide a separate Reverse MRP-to-Grams mode. The user enters: - Product - Variety - Pack Type - Desired MRP including GST - Applicable costs and margins The application automatically calculates: - Exact mathematically calculated pack weight - Commercially rounded pack weight - Maximum feasible weight at the target MRP - Relevant costing breakdown The user must NOT manually enter pack weight in this mode. Show the exact result and commercially rounded result separately. If the desired MRP cannot support the minimum practical pack size, show a clear warning. Use an accurate mathematical solution based on the same calculation engine as Cost-to-MRP. **IMPORTANT: This Reverse MRP-to-Grams feature is ONLY for regular products. It must not be available for Ladi / Multi-Pouch products or their price list.** --- ## 13. SEPARATE LADI / MULTI-POUCH PRODUCT CATEGORY Create a separate category and tab: **LADI / MULTI-POUCH PRODUCTS** Ladi is a separate commercial product variety. Ladi-Based Weight and Cost Calculation Ladi costing must be based on the actual number of individual pouches contained in one complete Ladi. Example: - Individual Pouch Weight = 5g - Number of Pouches per Ladi = 20 - Total Net Weight per Ladi = 100g Formula: Total Net Weight per Ladi = Individual Pouch Weight × Number of Pouches. Calculate: 1. Raw Material Cost per Ladi = Cost of the total 100g usable raw material. 2. Individual Pouch Packaging Cost = Cost per pouch × 20. 3. Individual Pouch Label Cost = Cost per label × 20. 4. Individual Pouch Printing Cost = Cost per pouch × 20. 5. Outer Ladi Packaging Cost = Actual cost of one complete Ladi's outer packaging. 6. Applicable Manufacturing / Processing / Grinding Cost. 7. Other applicable business costs. Total Ladi Cost = Sum of all applicable costs. Apply wastage, freight, marketing, marketing fund, scheme recovery, margins and GST using the same centralized business settings and calculation rules. **CRITICAL:** Never calculate Ladi MRP by simply copying one individual pouch's price or multiplying its MRP without calculating the complete Ladi cost. Calculate the complete Ladi independently using total weight, actual pouch quantity, all individual pouch packaging costs and outer Ladi packaging costs. ### C. Individual Pouch Pricing AND Complete Ladi Pricing For every Ladi product, maintain TWO independent commercial pricing structures. **1. Individual Pouch Pricing** Calculate and save: - Individual Pouch Weight - Raw Material Cost per Pouch - Wastage Cost per Pouch - Individual Pouch Packaging Cost - Label Cost per Pouch - Printing Cost per Pouch - Applicable Manufacturing Cost per Pouch - Total Cost per Pouch - Manufacturer Selling Price per Pouch - Distributor Price per Pouch - Retailer Selling Price per Pouch - MRP per Individual Pouch **2. Individual pouch pricing must represent ONE individual pouch. Ladi pricing must represent ONE COMPLETE LADI containing the specified number of individual pouches. Allow commercial price overrides where required, with a clear override indicator and complete pricing history. ### D.
